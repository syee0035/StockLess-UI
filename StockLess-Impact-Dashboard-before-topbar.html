Understanding your impact

Learn how these numbers are calculated and what they mean.

♻️How is overstock avoided estimated?

For each product StockLess could judge, it takes the stock you would hold after your planned order and subtracts the top of the expected four-week demand range. Anything above that top figure is stock you are unlikely to sell inside four weeks. Products without both a demand estimate and a stock figure are left out rather than counted as zero.

💰How is the cost saving calculated?

Each overstock unit is multiplied by the unit cost you mapped in Step 2. Products whose unit cost Step 3 could not confirm are left out of the total, never priced at a guess.

🌍What does CO₂e mean, and why is it blank?

CO₂e is carbon dioxide equivalent — a single figure covering several greenhouse gases. UNEP attributes 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions to food loss and waste, but that is a global share, not a rate per kilogram. Converting your plan into CO₂e would need the weight of each unit, and a StockLess file records units, never weights.

📈Are these figures exact?

No. They are estimates based on the sales, stock and purchase data in your file, and they are meant to support your decision rather than to report a verified outcome. A longer and cleaner sales history gives a steadier estimate; a short or patchy one gives a rougher estimate.

Method: estimates use the demand range StockLess worked out from your sales rows, the stock figures in your file, and the order quantities you entered. Figures change when your file or your planned orders change.
